> Stray thoughts land here. Tag them and the *"process ideas"* routine routes them to their destination file. Untagged bullets stay put — no inference, no auto-routing.

> Routine: `briefs/_IDEAS-PROCESSING.md`. Trigger phrase: *"process ideas"*.

---

## How to use

1. Drop a bullet here whenever an idea lands. One bullet = one thought. Don't pre-organize.
2. If you know where it belongs, append a hashtag (see Tag table below). The orchestrator routes it on the next *"process ideas"* run.
3. If you don't know where it belongs, leave it untagged. It'll surface in weekly maintenance and you can decide then.
4. Don't restructure this file. Sections grow chronologically; nothing here needs to be tidy.
